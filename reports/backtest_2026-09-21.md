## What this backtest does not tell you

- **E5 (AI veto) always passes.** News as of a past date cannot be reproduced.
- **Cash earns 0%.** This slightly understates the strategy in risk-off periods.
- **Costs** per trade: $0.005/share (min $1.00), 5 bps slippage, 3 bps spread (half crossed per side).
- **Signals at the close, fills at the next open.** No same-bar execution.
- **Universe is point-in-time** (top 150 by 20-day dollar volume among current constituents, spec 2.3). Still biased: today's S&P 500 membership list (survivorship), and sectors and security type taken from today's labels.
- **Risk engine order-level checks are not applied** (order caps, drawdown halt).
- **In-sample years are warm-up only.** Nothing is fitted; parameters are identical in every window.

- signals start 2005-11-01; first out-of-sample window 2008-11-03
- 0 orders had no bar to fill on; 0 weeks had an unsizable book; 0 entries dropped by E7
- 0 symbols have no sector and can never pass E8
